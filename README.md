## Overview

A personal analytics project applying the same rigor as a business analysis to self-tracked reading data, 27 books logged over a 13-month period (December 2024 – December 2025), covering title, author, page count, format, ownership, personal rating, and author nationality.

> **Business question:** What do my reading habits from December 2024 to December 2025 reveal about my consistency, preferences, and patterns as a reader, and what specific changes would help me read more consistently and diversely going forward?

## Key Findings

- **The habit is fairly consistent, but has two real gap months.** 27 books were read across 11 of 13 months (85% active-month rate). May 2025 and November 2025 had zero books logged.
- **One author accounts for over a fifth of everything read.** Gbile Akanni was read 6 times — 22% of all books — meaning the "diversity" picture looks stronger at the nationality level (7 countries) than at the author level.
- **Book length has almost no relationship with rating** (correlation ≈ 0.11) — but 2-star and 5-star books both had higher average page counts than 3- and 4-star books, a mild U-shape worth watching, not concluding.
- **Nigerian-authored books are read most often but rated slightly lower on average** — flagged as a pattern to track with more data, not a proven difference yet.
- **The most-repeated authors are also the most reliably highly-rated.** Every Gbile Akanni and Watchman Nee book was rated 4 or 5 — a genuine trusted-author signal, unlike the more tentative patterns above.

Full write-up with supporting numbers is in the **Insights** sheet.
